# Legal and Compliance Readiness

This checklist identifies where qualified legal counsel is required before launch. It does not provide legal conclusions. Every item marked counsel must be reviewed and signed off by a lawyer qualified in the relevant country (Benin and Togo for the pilot, both OHADA and UEMOA member states using the XOF currency).

## Contracts and terms

- [ ] Terms of Service reviewed by counsel (EN and FR). Owner: Founder plus counsel.
- [ ] Privacy Policy reviewed by counsel (EN and FR). Owner: Founder plus counsel.
- [ ] Influencer agreement reviewed by counsel. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Business agreement reviewed by counsel. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Collaboration contract template reviewed by counsel. The current in-app template carries a placeholder banner (NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED / MODELE PROVISOIRE) and that banner must be removed only after counsel sign off. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Refund and cancellation policy reviewed by counsel. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Dispute resolution and governing law clause set per market. Owner: counsel.

## Payments and money handling

- [ ] Confirm whether holding funds in escrow requires a payment or e-money license or a licensed partner in Benin and Togo. Owner: counsel. This is a launch blocker for real payments.
- [ ] Confirm cross border payout treatment and any reporting duties. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Tax responsibilities: platform commission invoicing, VAT or equivalent, and creator income reporting expectations. Owner: counsel plus finance.

## Data protection

- [ ] Map personal data collected (identity documents, phone, payment identifiers) and confirm the lawful basis and retention period. Owner: Founder plus counsel.
- [ ] Confirm data protection registration or notification duties in Benin (APDP) and Togo. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Confirm requirements for storing encrypted identity documents and who may access them. Owner: counsel plus engineering.

## Advertising and content

- [ ] Advertising disclosure rules for sponsored content per market. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Image and content ownership and license terms between creators and businesses. Owner: counsel.
- [ ] Minors: confirm the platform is restricted to users 18 and over and that this is enforced at registration. Owner: Product plus counsel.

## Brand

- [ ] Trademark registry search for Kolabo through counsel (OAPI covering Benin and Togo, plus Ghana and Nigeria for later markets). Owner: counsel. Outcome feeds the brand and logo spend decision.

## Founder approval required

- [ ] Governing law and dispute venue choice.
- [ ] Whether to launch payments via a licensed partner or defer real payments until a license path is clear.
