# Support and Operations Readiness

The pilot runs with manual processes on purpose. This list makes those manual processes explicit so nothing depends on one person remembering a step. It pairs with the admin dashboard and queues built in Epic B, Epic G, Epic H, and Epic I.

## Support channels

- [ ] Decide the pilot support channels (for example WhatsApp, email) and publish them in both languages. Owner: Community plus support.
- [ ] Support hours and expected response time set and communicated. Owner: Support.
- [ ] Bilingual support coverage confirmed (EN and FR). Owner: Support.

## Standard operating procedures

Write a short SOP for each of these so any operator can follow it.

- [ ] Verifying an influencer or business (reviewing documents and social accounts in the admin queue, approving or rejecting with a reason). Owner: Support.
- [ ] Handling a payment dispute (freeze, gather evidence from both sides, resolve as release or refund, all logged). Owner: Support plus finance.
- [ ] Acting on a fraud or abuse report from the trust and safety queue (triage, decide, log). Owner: Support plus trust and safety.
- [ ] Manual payout confirmation during the pilot if the provider integration is not yet automated. Owner: Finance.
- [ ] Escalation path from support to founder for anything involving money, legal risk, or a safety issue. Owner: Support.

## Daily operations

- [ ] The admin dashboard is the start of day view: verification queue, open disputes, held and overdue payouts, open reports. Owner: Support.
- [ ] A daily check that overdue escrows auto released as expected. Owner: Finance.
- [ ] A simple log of decisions and issues during the pilot for the post launch review. Owner: Support.

## Founder approval required

- [ ] Sign off the support model and hours for the pilot.
